# HypeRate

Your **real heart rate**, live in Geometry Dash.

Connect any device supported by [HypeRate](https://www.hyperate.io) (smartwatches, chest straps, fitness bands) and watch your pulse while you play.

## Setup
1. Install the HypeRate app and connect your device.
2. Copy your **HypeRate ID**.
3. Open the mod settings and paste your ID. That's it!

No sensor yet? Turn on **Demo Mode** to try everything with a simulated heart rate.

## Features
- <cg>Beating heart</c> that pulses at your real heart rate
- <cy>Zone colors</c> from calm to panic, with your own thresholds
- <co>Drag & drop HUD editor</c> - press the heart button in the pause menu
- <cr>Ice Cold Mode</c> - reach your heart rate limit and you die. Stay calm!
- Automatic reconnect and clear status messages

## Privacy
The mod only **receives** your heart rate from HypeRate using your ID. It does not send any gameplay data anywhere.
