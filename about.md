# HypeRate

Ever wondered what your heart does on that one jump at 94%? Now you can see it.

This is the official <cr>HypeRate</c> mod. It puts your **live heart rate** right into Geometry Dash and shows you exactly where in a level you start to panic. It works with pretty much any smartwatch, fitness band or chest strap that you can connect to the HypeRate app.

## Getting started
1. Install the HypeRate app and connect your device.
2. Open this mod's settings, paste your **HypeRate ID** and press **Apply**.
3. The status line under your ID turns <cg>green</c> once your heart rate comes in. That's it!

No sensor at hand? Turn on **Demo Mode** and try everything with a simulated heart rate.

## Your heart rate while you play
A little heart in the corner beats along with your real pulse and changes from <cg>green</c> to <cy>yellow</c> to <cr>red</c> as things get tense. What counts as tense is up to your own heart: the mod learns your normal heart rate while you play and sets the colors around it.

Want it somewhere else? Press the heart button in the pause menu, then **Edit HUD**, and drag it wherever you like. You can pick from four heart styles in the settings, and when no heart rate comes in, the display simply fades out until your sensor is back.

## The Heart Map
Every attempt is recorded against your progress through the level. Press the heart button in the pause menu (or at the end of a level) to see your pulse from 0 to 100%, your peak and your average.

Over time a colored strip under the progress bar shows where you **always** get nervous. The mod also remembers your best run, your highest heart rate and your calmest clear for every level.

## Ice Cold Mode
For the brave ones: set a heart rate limit and if you go over it, you die. It only ever makes the game harder, never easier.

## Privacy
The mod only **receives** your heart rate from HypeRate using your ID. It doesn't send anything about your gameplay anywhere. Your level stats are saved on your device and nowhere else.
