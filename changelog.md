# v1.0.0
First release!
- Live heart rate from HypeRate, just enter your HypeRate ID
- Heart rate display with a heart that beats along with your pulse
- Automatic zones: learns your normal heart rate and colors the display from calm to panic
- Four heart styles to choose from
- HUD editor in the pause menu for position, size and opacity
- Heart Map: your heart rate over the level progress, with peak, average and an all-time heatmap
- Heatmap strip under the progress bar
- Saved stats per level: best run, highest heart rate and calmest clear
- Ice Cold Mode: go over your heart rate limit and you die
- Demo Mode to try everything without a sensor
- Live connection status in the settings
